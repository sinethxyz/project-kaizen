# Market Map

This map is conceptual and should be updated as the market changes.

| Player / category | Typical starting point | Main objective | Embedded operators? | AI assumed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI transformation firms | AI opportunity | AI-enabled / AI-native enterprise | Often | Usually |
| Forward-deployed AI teams | frontier AI | production deployment | Yes | Yes |
| Systems integrators | enterprise systems | integration / transformation | Often | No |
| Large consultancies | business problem | broad transformation | Mixed | No |
| Palantir | enterprise data + operations | operational intelligence | Yes | Less |
| Rippling | worker identity / employee data | compound workforce/business software | Product-led | No |
| Project Kaizen thesis | enterprise itself | continuous adaptation | Yes | No |

The objective is not to pretend Project Kaizen has no competitors. It is to test whether starting from the enterprise itself produces a meaningfully different company and customer experience.
