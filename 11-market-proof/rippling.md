# Rippling

Rippling is useful as an architectural reference, not as a direct competitor analogy.

## Strategic lesson

Rippling's platform has long centred worker/employee identity and shared data across multiple workforce applications. Its published 2024 investor memo describes the evolution of the company's strategy over time.

In June 2026, Rippling expanded this logic with Rippling Data Cloud. Rippling describes worker identity as a key context for business questions and connects external business data with workforce context. Its Data Catalog provides a governed inventory of data objects for both humans and AI, including lineage, permissions, and meaning.

Simplified:

```text
WORKER / EMPLOYEE
↓
SHARED DATA + CONTEXT
↓
COMMON CAPABILITIES
↓
MULTIPLE APPLICATIONS / ANALYTICS / AI
```

## Project Kaizen analogy

```text
ENTERPRISE
↓
ENTERPRISE REALITY MODEL
↓
SHARED UNDERSTANDING
↓
DIAGNOSIS + TRANSFORMATION
```

The useful lesson is:

> **Look beneath fragmented categories for the primitive and context they share.**

The analogy breaks because `the enterprise` is far broader and less concrete than `worker identity`. Project Kaizen should discover reusable primitives through actual transformations rather than assuming them.
