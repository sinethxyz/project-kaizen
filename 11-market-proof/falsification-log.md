# Falsification Log

Use this file to record evidence that weakens Project Kaizen.

Template:

```text
DATE:

CLAIM:

NEW EVIDENCE:

WHY IT MATTERS:

DOES IT WEAKEN, REFRAME, OR DISPROVE THE CLAIM?

MODEL UPDATE:
```

Examples of potentially falsifying evidence:

- technology-specific firms consistently outperform neutral diagnosticians
- buyers refuse to pay for diagnosis without a predetermined implementation
- cross-enterprise patterns fail to generalise enough to compound
- contractor routing produces lower quality than full-time specialist teams
- AI becomes so foundational that treating it as a downstream capability obscures reality
- `continuous enterprise adaptation` proves too broad to sell or operationalise

A thesis that cannot lose is not a useful thesis.
