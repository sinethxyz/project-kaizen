# Organisational Sediment

Enterprise complexity often accumulates historically:

```text
problem
↓
new process
↓
spreadsheet
↓
software
↓
exception
↓
acquisition
↓
approval layer
↓
employee leaves
↓
original reason forgotten
↓
complex enterprise
```

Not all complexity is waste. Some reflects real regulation, risk, or domain requirements.

The diagnostic task is to distinguish necessary complexity from historical sediment.
