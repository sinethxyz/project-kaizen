# Blockbuster vs Netflix

## Wave
Digital distribution.

## Surface interpretation
Movies became available online.

## Deeper interpretation
Distribution, subscription economics, convenience, recommendation, physical-location economics, and eventually content strategy changed.

## Pattern
> **Adding a new technology to an organisation still optimised around old economics may be insufficient.**

Netflix itself repeatedly changed its operating model; the useful lesson is adaptation, not a simplistic `Blockbuster ignored streaming` story.
