# AI — The Case Study Being Written Now

The outcome is unknown because we are living inside the transition.

Possible enterprise responses include:

## Denial

```text
AI does not matter
↓
ignore genuine structural changes
```

## Technology worship

```text
AI changes everything
↓
deploy everywhere
↓
business diagnosis disappears
```

## Diagnosis

```text
What has AI actually changed?
↓
Which economics changed?
↓
Which enterprise capabilities are misaligned?
↓
What should change?
↓
Where does AI genuinely help?
```

Project Kaizen argues for the third pattern, but whether it proves superior must be tested empirically.
