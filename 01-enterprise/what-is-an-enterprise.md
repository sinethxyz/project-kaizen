# What Is an Enterprise?

At first principles, an enterprise turns inputs into value through information, decisions, coordination, and action.

```text
INPUTS
↓
INFORMATION
↓
DECISIONS
↓
COORDINATION
↓
ACTIONS
↓
OUTPUTS
↓
VALUE
↓
FEEDBACK
↺
```

Real enterprises are messier because they contain history, regulation, incentives, politics, technology, culture, and imperfect humans.

The goal of enterprise intelligence is not to remove that mess from the model. It is to understand enough of it to explain outcomes and improve decisions.
