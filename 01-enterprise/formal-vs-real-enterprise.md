# Formal vs Real Enterprise

Every organisation has at least two representations.

## Formal enterprise

- org chart
- official processes
- policies
- KPIs
- approved systems
- documented ownership
- stated decision rights

## Real enterprise

- who people actually ask
- who can quietly veto
- which spreadsheet runs the process
- who owns undocumented knowledge
- where approvals really happen
- which metric leadership actually trusts
- which policies are ignored
- which relationships carry influence

Transformation fails when it changes the formal enterprise but not the real one.

A diagnosis should therefore ask not only **what is supposed to happen**, but **what actually happens and why**.
