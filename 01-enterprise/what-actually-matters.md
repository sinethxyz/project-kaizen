# What Actually Matters?

Across technological waves, a few enterprise qualities remain consistently valuable.

## Truth

Can the organisation accurately perceive reality?

## Coherence

Do people, systems, data, processes, and metrics describe the same organisation?

## Decision quality

Can relevant information become good decisions at the right level and time?

## Execution

Can decisions reliably become actions and outcomes?

## Adaptability

Can the organisation update itself when technology, markets, regulation, or internal conditions change?

Technology serves these capabilities. It is not the end state.
