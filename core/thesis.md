# Thesis

## Centre of gravity

> **The enterprise is the centre of gravity. Technology orbits the enterprise.**

AI, cloud, ERP, SaaS, automation, robotics, and whatever comes next are capabilities whose relevance depends on the enterprise and its environment.

The enterprise evolves, but it remains the enduring object of understanding.

That is the sense in which Project Kaizen uses:

> **The enterprise is the invariant. AI is a variable.**

## Persist mission

> **Understand enterprises deeply enough to know what any new technology actually means for them and how to debug or close the gaps inside the enterprise.**

The mission has two halves.

**Interpretation:** understand what a change in technology, market, regulation, economics, or competition actually changes for a specific enterprise.

**Intervention:** identify the gap between how the enterprise works now and how it needs to work, then close the right gap.

```text
ENTERPRISE
↓
What creates value?
↓
How does the system actually work?
↓
What changed?
↓
Where is the gap?
↓
What should change?
↓
Which capability closes it?
```

## Sineth's abstraction

A useful strategic heuristic is:

```text
ENTERPRISE
↓
determines requirements
↓
TECHNOLOGY
↓
changes possibilities
↓
ENTERPRISE EVOLVES
↺
```

Calling the enterprise the independent variable and AI a dependent variable is useful as a thinking tool, not as a literal causal or statistical law.

The practical rule is simple:

> **Do not assume the technology before understanding the enterprise.**

## Five lines

> **Technologies change.**  
> **Markets change.**  
> **Systems change.**  
> **Companies either adapt or die.**  
> **Persist.**

Project Kaizen is the mindset and method.

Persist is the outcome.
