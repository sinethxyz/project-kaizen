# Boundaries

Project Kaizen becomes weaker if it expands into a theory of everything.

## What it is not

It is not a mandate to:

- offer every technology or service
- employ every kind of specialist
- automate every decision
- replace management
- model every human emotion
- treat AI output as fact
- perform amateur psychological diagnosis on founders or employees
- prescribe constant change
- turn every engagement into a large transformation programme

## Operational focus can stay narrow

Persist can spend years primarily selling AI modernisation.

```text
WHAT WE SELL TODAY
≠
WHAT WE MUST ALWAYS BE
```

The internal philosophy can remain broad while the commercial wedge remains narrow.

## Observable behaviour over labels

When analysing people or power, prefer observable statements:

- decisions remain centralised
- one executive controls critical information
- approvals repeatedly stall in one function
- employees route around the formal process

Avoid claims about motives, personalities, or mental states without evidence.

## Evidence before inference

Always distinguish:

```text
EVIDENCE
≠
INFERENCE
≠
SPECULATION
```

## Simplicity test

Before adding another concept, ask:

> **Does this make the reasoning logic clearer, more falsifiable, or more executable?**

If not, leave it out.
