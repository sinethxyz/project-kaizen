# Positioning

## External wedge

Persist can initially be sold in a narrow and legible way, for example around AI modernisation or enterprise modernisation.

A useful external explanation is:

> We help companies modernise for the AI era, but we do not begin with AI. We begin with the business, diagnose what actually needs to change, modernise the relevant system, and deploy AI only where it creates measurable value.

## Internal identity

Internally, the company should not confuse its current wedge with its permanent identity.

```text
AI MODERNISATION
= current commercial wedge

CONTINUOUS ENTERPRISE ADAPTATION
= deeper internal philosophy
```

## Category difference

A technology led model often asks:

```text
TECHNOLOGY
↓
USE CASES
↓
IMPLEMENTATION
```

Project Kaizen asks:

```text
ENTERPRISE
↓
DIAGNOSIS
↓
INTERVENTION
↓
TECHNOLOGY IF USEFUL
```

## Do not overclaim

This is not automatically a blue ocean.

Digital transformation, systems integration, management consulting, cloud modernisation, automation, AI transformation, and embedded engineering already occupy adjacent territory.

The model becomes strategically meaningful only if it produces a different customer experience, economics, outcome quality, or learning advantage.

## Tenex contrast

Tenex is useful as a live contrast because it explicitly chooses AI transformation as its category.

That is coherent for Tenex.

Project Kaizen simply chooses a different starting assumption: the enterprise, rather than AI, owns the problem space.
