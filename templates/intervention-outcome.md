# Intervention and Outcome Record

## Context

What enterprise state and problem existed before the intervention?

## Diagnosis

What root constraint or opportunity was identified?

## Hypothesis

What did we expect to change, and why?

## Baseline

What measurable or observable state existed before deployment?

## Intervention

What changed?

Who owned it?

Which capabilities were involved?

What did it cost in time, money, and organisational effort?

## Outcome

What changed after deployment?

Which expected outcomes occurred?

Which did not?

## Learning

What assumption was wrong?

What hidden variable mattered?

Would we recommend this intervention again?

Under what conditions?

What should update in the enterprise model, routing logic, or pattern library?
