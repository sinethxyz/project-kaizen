# Measurement

Transformation without measurement becomes storytelling.

Before deployment, define:

- baseline
- target outcome
- expected mechanism
- leading indicators
- lagging indicators
- cost
- time horizon
- failure conditions

After deployment, ask:

- what changed?
- what did not?
- which assumptions were wrong?
- what unexpected behaviour appeared?
- was the change economically worthwhile?
- would we recommend the same intervention again under the same conditions?

Measurement should improve both the client and Persist's model.
