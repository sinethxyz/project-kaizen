# Learning Loop

Learning is not a retrospective document. It is a model update.

```text
OBSERVATION
↓
HYPOTHESIS
↓
INTERVENTION
↓
OUTCOME
↓
MODEL UPDATE
```

Capture:

- what was believed before
- what evidence supported it
- what intervention followed
- what happened
- what was surprising
- what should be changed in future diagnosis or routing

Do not generalise from one client without sufficient evidence.
