# Minimum Sufficient Intervention

Persist should not optimise for the largest transformation it can sell.

It should optimise for the **smallest intervention that materially improves the enterprise**.

> **Understand deeply. Change only what needs changing.**

The purpose of diagnosis is not to manufacture work. It is to reduce uncertainty about what actually matters.

A valid diagnosis may conclude that the enterprise needs:

- one workflow redesigned rather than an organisation-wide programme;
- one source of truth rather than a new AI layer;
- one ownership decision rather than new software;
- one specialist for two weeks rather than a permanent team;
- a controlled experiment rather than a full deployment;
- or no intervention at all.

## Default sequence

```text
OBSERVE
↓
FIND THE CONSTRAINT
↓
IDENTIFY THE MINIMUM CHANGE
↓
TEST
↓
MEASURE
↓
EXPAND ONLY IF JUSTIFIED
```

Large programmes create more cost, organisational resistance, coordination overhead, and ways to be wrong.

Therefore the burden of proof should increase with the size and irreversibility of the intervention.

## Test

Before recommending an intervention, ask:

1. What specific enterprise condition are we trying to change?
2. What is the smallest credible intervention that could change it?
3. What evidence would tell us it worked?
4. Can we test the hypothesis before committing more resources?
5. What happens if we do nothing?

The objective is not maximum transformation activity.

The objective is a better enterprise with the least unnecessary change.
