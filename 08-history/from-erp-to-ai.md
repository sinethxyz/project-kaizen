# From ERP to AI

Enterprise technology history repeatedly produces a similar pattern:

1. a new capability becomes possible
2. early adopters gain leverage
3. vendors build categories around the capability
4. enterprises reorganise around it
5. the capability normalises
6. attention moves to the next constraint

ERP, the web, SaaS, cloud, data infrastructure, ML, and AI are not identical. Their economics and depth of impact differ.

The reusable lesson is not `all trends are the same`.

It is:

> **Do not mistake the current modernisation programme for the permanent capability an enterprise needs.**
