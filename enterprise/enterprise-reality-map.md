# Enterprise Reality Map

The Enterprise Reality Map is the working representation of how the company actually functions.

It should be descriptive before it becomes prescriptive.

## Map

Capture:

- business model and value creation
- customers and demand
- organisational structure and ownership
- systems and integrations
- data and canonical entities
- important workflows
- decision rights and approval paths
- economics and constraints
- incentives
- formal and informal power
- dependencies and single points of failure
- historical decisions that still shape the company
- major technologies
- current external changes
- observed gaps, contradictions, and unknowns

## Trace symptoms downward

A useful diagnostic path is:

```text
SYMPTOM
↓
PROCESS
↓
INFORMATION
↓
SYSTEM
↓
ORGANISATION
↓
INCENTIVES / POWER / HISTORY
↓
ROOT CONSTRAINT
```

Ask:

> **Where does the problem first become inevitable?**

A request such as "we need an AI support agent" may ultimately trace to fragmented customer identity, poor knowledge ownership, broken escalation rules, or something else entirely.

## Separate state from interpretation

Record what is known separately from what is inferred.

Useful states:

```text
KNOWN
LIKELY
CONTESTED
UNKNOWN
DISPROVEN
```

The map should become more accurate over time as interviews, systems, behaviour, outcomes, and new evidence update it.
