# Enterprise Stack

The stack is a diagnostic heuristic, not a natural law.

```text
L1   Connectivity
L2   Data foundations
L3   Organisation and ownership
L4   Observability
L5   Analytics
L6   Process design
L7   Decision systems
L8   Automation
L9   Prediction and optimisation
L10  AI and generative intelligence
L11  Adaptive and autonomous systems
```

## Rule

> **A visible problem at a high layer may have its root cause much lower down.**

Example:

```text
"We need an AI support agent"
↓
support is slow
↓
staff search several systems
↓
customer records are fragmented
↓
there is no canonical customer identity
```

The visible request may sit at L10 while the root issue sits near L2 or L3.

Use the stack to prevent premature technology diagnosis, not to force every company into a rigid hierarchy.
