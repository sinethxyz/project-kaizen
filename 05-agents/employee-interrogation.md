# Employee Interrogation

Frontline workers often reveal the real enterprise faster than formal process documents.

Ask:

- Which part of your job feels unnecessarily difficult?
- Which task requires the most copying and pasting?
- Which information is hardest to find?
- Which process do you ignore because it does not work?
- Which spreadsheet is secretly critical?
- What do managers misunderstand about your work?
- What would break if one specific employee disappeared?
- What rule do people routinely work around?
- Which tool do you dislike most and why?
- What would you automate tomorrow?
- What should absolutely not be automated?

Protect employees from retaliation and avoid attributing sensitive comments unnecessarily.
