# Leadership Interrogation

Interview leaders independently before allowing their answers to converge socially.

Useful questions:

- What are the company's top three priorities?
- What prevents them from happening?
- Which other executive do you depend on most?
- Which function creates the most friction?
- Which metric do you distrust?
- Where does information arrive too late?
- Which decisions take absurdly long?
- Which decisions happen too quickly?
- What problem does everyone know about but nobody owns?
- Which initiative is politically difficult to challenge?

Differences between executive answers may themselves be evidence about alignment and enterprise coherence.
