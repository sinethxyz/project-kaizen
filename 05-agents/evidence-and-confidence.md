# Evidence and Confidence

Confidence should be attached to claims, not to the personality of the agent producing them.

For each important conclusion, record:

- source type
- recency
- independence of sources
- directness of evidence
- contradictory evidence
- confidence
- sensitivity to assumptions
- what evidence would change the conclusion

Do not let a model's fluency substitute for evidence quality.

The system should distinguish `management says X` from `workflow logs demonstrate X`.
