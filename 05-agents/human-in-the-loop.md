# Human in the Loop

AI agents should improve enterprise reasoning, not replace accountable human judgment.

Humans remain necessary for:

- interpreting ambiguous organisational context
- judging sensitive power and behavioural claims
- protecting confidentiality
- validating high-impact decisions
- recognising when evidence is missing
- communicating with affected people
- taking responsibility for deployment

Agent output should never be treated as established truth merely because multiple models agree.

Human review is especially important for claims about motives, emotions, individual performance, legal risk, or organisational politics.
