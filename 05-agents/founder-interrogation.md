# Founder Interrogation

Founder interviews should explore history, decision-making, power, and assumptions rather than using a generic consultancy script.

## Origins

- Why did you start the company?
- What did you believe about the market then?
- Which early assumptions were right?
- Which were wrong?
- What almost killed the company early?

## Decisions

- Which decisions still come to you?
- Which decisions should not come to you but still do?
- Which decisions will you not delegate?
- Who can change your mind?
- Who do you trust with bad news?
- What information do you personally check?

## Power

- Who has more influence than their title suggests?
- Who could quietly stop a major initiative?
- Who controls resources others depend on?
- Who would you be most worried about losing tomorrow?

## Culture

- What behaviour gets rewarded here?
- What gets punished even if nobody admits it?
- What can employees openly disagree with you about?
- Which subjects are politically difficult?

## History

- Which process exists because of something that happened years ago?
- Which system does everyone dislike but nobody replaces?
- Which organisational decision would you reverse if you could?

## Technology

- Which technology investments actually changed the company?
- Which disappointed you?
- What technology does management currently believe it needs, and why?

## Reality

- What do you think is the biggest problem?
- What do employees think is the biggest problem?
- What do customers think is the biggest problem?
- Where do those answers differ?

Dynamic questions generated from contradictions should take priority over this base list.
