# Meta-Agent

The meta-agent orchestrates disagreement rather than pretending to be the final oracle.

Its job is to identify:

- where agents agree
- where they disagree
- why they disagree
- which claims depend on weak evidence
- what new evidence would resolve important uncertainty
- which hypotheses should be killed
- which questions should be asked next

The strongest output may be:

> **We cannot responsibly decide yet. Collect these five pieces of evidence.**

The meta-agent should preserve minority arguments when they are evidence-rich rather than collapsing everything into consensus.
