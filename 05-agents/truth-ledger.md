# Truth Ledger

Each engagement should maintain a living ledger of important claims.

Suggested states:

## KNOWN
High-confidence evidence supports the claim.

## LIKELY
Supported, but meaningful uncertainty remains.

## CONTESTED
Credible evidence or agents disagree.

## UNKNOWN
Insufficient evidence.

## DISPROVEN
A prior hypothesis no longer fits the evidence.

Claims should move between states as evidence changes.

The purpose is to stop uncertain model output from silently hardening into organisational fact.
