# Pre-Mortem and Post-Mortem Agents

## Pre-mortem

Before deployment, assume the intervention failed badly twelve months from now.

Ask multiple agents to explain why.

Potential causes:

- employees rejected it
- data quality was worse than believed
- incentives were unchanged
- leadership stopped supporting it
- business case was wrong
- vendor became a bottleneck
- integration took too long
- regulation changed
- the technology was unnecessary

Use the failure stories to redesign the intervention.

## Post-mortem

After deployment, ask:

- what changed?
- what did not?
- which forecast was wrong?
- which hidden variable mattered?
- what unexpected behaviour emerged?
- would we recommend the same intervention again?
- under what conditions?

The goal is model improvement, not blame.
