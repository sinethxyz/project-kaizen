# Contradiction Mining

Contradictions are diagnostic assets.

Search across interviews, systems, policies, metrics, workflows, and behaviour.

Examples:

```text
"We decentralise decisions"
vs
79% of material contracts still require CEO approval
```

```text
"We are customer obsessed"
vs
support backlog is measured in weeks
```

```text
"Data drives decisions"
vs
leadership uses three incompatible revenue figures
```

```text
"We need AI"
vs
most required information is not digitised or owned
```

A contradiction should generate a new hypothesis and a more precise question.
