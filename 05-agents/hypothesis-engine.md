# Hypothesis Engine

Agents should produce hypotheses rather than final-sounding narratives.

Recommended structure:

```text
HYPOTHESIS ID:

Claim:

Evidence for:

Evidence against:

Confidence:

What would falsify it:

Evidence required next:

Potential consequence if true:
```

Example:

```text
Claim:
Customer-data fragmentation is primarily an ownership problem.

Evidence for:
Three teams maintain different customer definitions.
Previous integration was rejected by business-unit leaders.

Evidence against:
Schemas are technically incompatible.

Confidence:
0.63

Evidence required:
Interview business-unit heads and inspect incentive design.
```

Explicit uncertainty is preferable to polished certainty.
