# Learning From Enterprises

The long term opportunity is not merely a better prompt or a proprietary LLM.

It is an increasingly useful enterprise reasoning system.

## Flywheel

```text
MORE ENGAGEMENTS
↓
MORE OBSERVATIONS
↓
BETTER PATTERNS
↓
BETTER DIAGNOSIS
↓
BETTER ROUTING
↓
BETTER INTERVENTIONS
↓
BETTER OUTCOMES
↺
```

Over time the system may learn reusable patterns such as:

- symptom combinations that often imply a deeper constraint
- interventions that repeatedly fail under certain conditions
- prerequisites for successful automation or AI deployment
- capabilities that are repeatedly required in particular environments

## Do not overgeneralise

A pattern learned from a small number of companies is not a law.

Track scope, evidence, sample size, conditions, counterexamples, and confidence.

## Privacy

Cross enterprise learning must be lawful, contractually permitted, and designed around confidentiality.

Learn patterns without leaking client specifics.

The goal is:

> **Persist compounds its understanding of enterprises faster than technology changes around them.**
