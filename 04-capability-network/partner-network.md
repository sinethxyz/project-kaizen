# Partner Network

Persist's network can include organisations as well as individuals.

Potential partner categories:

- cloud and compute providers
- regional/sovereign infrastructure
- AI labs and model providers
- cybersecurity firms
- ERP and SaaS vendors
- universities and research groups
- privacy/legal specialists
- systems integrators
- hardware suppliers
- industry associations
- local operator firms

The network should remain plural rather than locking Persist to one vendor whose product then biases diagnosis.
