# Why Persist?

Project Kaizen describes the mindset and method.

Persist describes the outcome.

```text
KAIZEN
↓
CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
↓
ADAPTABILITY
↓
PERSISTENCE
```

The name is deliberately technology-agnostic.

It should still make sense if the dominant wave changes from AI to robotics, autonomy, a new computing paradigm, or something nobody currently predicts.

> **Technologies change. Markets change. Systems change. Companies either adapt or die. Persist.**
