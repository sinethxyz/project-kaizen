# The Enterprise Is the Centre of Gravity

Most AI transformation thinking places AI at the centre and asks how the enterprise should reorganise around it.

Project Kaizen reverses the direction:

> **The enterprise is the centre of gravity. Technology orbits the enterprise.**

AI, cloud, ERP, SaaS, automation, robotics, and whatever comes next are capabilities whose relevance depends on the enterprise and its environment.

The durable capability is:

> **Understand enterprises deeply enough to know what any new technology actually means for them.**

This does not make technology expertise less important. It changes its role. Technology expertise serves enterprise understanding rather than replacing it.

A useful sequence is:

```text
ENTERPRISE
↓
What creates value?
↓
How does the system actually work?
↓
What changed around it?
↓
Where is it misaligned?
↓
What should change?
↓
Which capability or technology helps?
```

AI is the current wave. The enterprise is the enduring object.
