# Four Principles

## Business first

Understand how the company creates and captures value, who the customer is, where margins come from, what the objectives are, and what is constraining performance.

## Systems second

Understand the people, processes, information, software, data, infrastructure, responsibilities, incentives, and dependencies producing those outcomes.

## Technology third

Technology is an intervention. It should follow understanding and diagnosis.

## AI somewhere much later

AI must earn its place.

Sometimes it will be transformational. Sometimes ordinary software, process redesign, data work, analytics, organisational change, or no intervention will be better.

> **Prescription follows diagnosis. Technology follows prescription.**
