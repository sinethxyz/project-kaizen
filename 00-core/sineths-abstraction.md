# Sineth's Abstraction

The intuitive strategic model is:

> **Enterprise = independent variable.**  
> **AI enablement = dependent variable.**

This is not strict statistical or causal terminology. It is a forcing function against solution bias.

Whether AI is useful depends on the state of the enterprise: its business model, systems, people, data, processes, economics, incentives, infrastructure, objectives, and constraints.

So:

```text
ENTERPRISE
↓
UNDERSTAND
↓
DIAGNOSE
↓
INTERVENTION
↓
TECHNOLOGY
```

Not:

```text
AI
↓
FIND USE CASES
```

The model is recursive because technology also changes what enterprises can become. The rule that survives is simple:

> **Do not distort the enterprise to justify the technology.**
