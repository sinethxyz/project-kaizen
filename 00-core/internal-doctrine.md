# Internal Doctrine

## This is a mindset, not a service catalogue

Project Kaizen does not mean Persist should operationally provide every possible technology or transformation capability.

Persist may remain extremely focused on AI modernisation for years.

The distinction is:

```text
WHAT WE CURRENTLY SELL
≠
WHAT WE PERMANENTLY BELIEVE
```

Operational specialisation is good. Intellectual rigidity is not.

## The trap

Companies often move through this sequence:

```text
We are good at X
↓
Customers pay us for X
↓
We become an X company
↓
X becomes our identity
↓
Reality changes
↓
We still need X to be the answer
```

Project Kaizen is a guardrail against that progression.

The permanent principles should be closer to:

- understand reality
- diagnose before prescribing
- solve the business problem rather than justify a technology
- measure whether the intervention worked
- learn
- change when reality changes

Persist should apply this doctrine to itself.
