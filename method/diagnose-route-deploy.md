# Diagnose, Route, Deploy

The operating logic is:

```text
ENTERPRISE
↓
UNDERSTAND
↓
DIAGNOSE
↓
ROUTE
↓
DEPLOY
↓
MEASURE
↓
LEARN
↺
```

## Understand

Build enough of the Enterprise Reality Map to understand the business, system, constraints, incentives, history, and external change.

## Diagnose

Do not begin with a solution category.

Ask what is actually producing the symptom and what would need to change for the outcome to improve.

A valid diagnosis may conclude:

```text
SOMETHING BROKEN
→ fix it

FOUNDATIONS HEALTHY
→ explore automation, AI, optimisation, or growth

NOTHING URGENT
→ do nothing unnecessary
```

## Route

Once the constraint is understood, determine the capability required.

The answer may be internal expertise, a specialist, partner, vendor, or temporary operator pod.

## Deploy

Persist should own the outcome, not merely hand over a recommendation.

Design, build, integrate, change the workflow, support adoption, and manage the intervention as required.

## Measure

Establish a baseline before intervention where possible.

Measure what actually changed rather than counting activity.

## Learn

Update the enterprise model, intervention logic, capability routing, and reusable patterns based on the outcome.

The method itself is subject to the same rule:

> **If diagnose, route, deploy stops being the best method, change it.**
