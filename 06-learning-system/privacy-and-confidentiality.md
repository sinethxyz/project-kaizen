# Privacy and Confidentiality

Compounding enterprise intelligence must never mean leaking client data across tenants.

Core principles:

- strict tenant isolation
- least-privilege access
- explicit data ownership
- contractual clarity on learning rights
- retention limits
- auditable access
- encryption and security controls
- anonymisation or aggregation where appropriate
- client-specific models remain client-specific unless permission says otherwise
- no model output should expose another client's internal facts

Sensitive organisational information can include commercial secrets, employee data, legal material, internal politics, and strategic plans.

The learning system should be designed around these constraints from the beginning rather than treating them as an afterthought.
