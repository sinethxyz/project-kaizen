# Reading List

Project Kaizen should become a student of enterprises, not merely technology.

Build a serious reading programme across:

## Systems
- systems thinking
- cybernetics
- complex adaptive systems
- systems dynamics

## Organisations
- organisational theory
- organisational behaviour
- culture
- incentives
- power and governance
- change management

## Economics and decisions
- microeconomics
- transaction-cost economics
- game theory
- behavioural economics
- decision science
- operations research

## Enterprise technology
- enterprise architecture
- databases
- ERP
- cloud
- data engineering
- software architecture
- cybersecurity
- AI/ML systems

## Operations
- process design
- Lean
- Toyota Production System
- Theory of Constraints
- Kaizen

## Strategy and history
- technological transitions
- disruption
- corporate adaptation
- capital allocation
- platform strategy

Specific books/papers should be added only after review, with notes on what each contributes to the model.
