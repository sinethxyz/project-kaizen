# Kodak

Kodak matters because the company was not simply unaware of digital imaging.

The more interesting problem is what happens when a new technology attacks the economics, incentives, and organisational commitments of the existing business.

## Pattern
> **Technological awareness does not automatically produce organisational adaptation.**

Use the case to study path dependence and business-model conflict rather than repeating the myth that Kodak merely failed to invent or notice digital photography.
