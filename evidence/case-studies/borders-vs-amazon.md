# Borders vs Amazon

## Wave
Internet / e-commerce.

## Surface interpretation
Books could be sold online.

## Deeper interpretation
The Internet affected discovery, distribution, convenience, inventory economics, scale, and consumer behaviour.

## Possible Project Kaizen pattern
> **Structural change can be mistaken for another channel.**

## Caution
Borders' outcome cannot be reduced to one technology decision. Capital structure, execution, management, competition, and timing also matter. Sources and counterarguments should be maintained in `13-sources/`.
