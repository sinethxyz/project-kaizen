# Technology Becomes Infrastructure

A technology can move through several strategic phases:

```text
NOVEL
↓
SCARCE CAPABILITY
↓
STRATEGIC DIFFERENTIATOR
↓
WIDESPREAD ADOPTION
↓
EXPECTED INFRASTRUCTURE
```

This does not mean the technology stops mattering. It means the basis of differentiation can move elsewhere.

The risk for a technology-defined company is that its category label loses strategic importance even while the underlying capability remains useful.

Project Kaizen is designed to reduce dependence on one category remaining scarce forever.
