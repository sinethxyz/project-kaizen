# Industry Direction

Track developments that could strengthen or weaken Project Kaizen.

## Operator models

Forward-deployed engineering is spreading across AI and enterprise software. This weakens any claim that embedded operators alone are novel.

## Enterprise context before AI

Palantir's Ontology architecture and Rippling's Data Cloud/Data Catalog both emphasise context, governed data, and real-world objects underneath AI-enabled workflows.

## AI transformation as current demand

Tenex and OpenAI's deployment efforts demonstrate that organisations are paying for help turning AI into operational systems.

## Questions to keep updating

- does AI become normal infrastructure faster than expected?
- do AI transformation firms broaden beyond AI?
- do consultancies build strong agentic/diagnostic systems?
- do customers pay for neutral diagnosis separately from implementation?
- do sovereign/local compute and regulation materially change routing?
- does robotics become another major enterprise transformation wave?
- do specialist networks outperform vertically integrated teams?

This file should evolve continuously rather than becoming a frozen 2026 market snapshot.
