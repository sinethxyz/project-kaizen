# Forward-Deployed Engineering

The operator thesis is increasingly validated by the market.

Palantir describes Forward Deployed Engineering as a methodology where engineers work close to customer problems and feed field learning back into core engineering.

In May 2026, OpenAI announced the **OpenAI Deployment Company**, designed to embed Forward Deployed Engineers into organisations to identify where AI can create impact, redesign infrastructure/workflows, and deploy durable systems. OpenAI also announced an agreement to acquire Tomoro in connection with the launch.

Tenex likewise hires Forward Deployed Engineers and uses dedicated engineering pods.

## Implication for Project Kaizen

`Operators, not recommendation-only consultants` is not unique.

The possible differentiation is the combination of:

- embedded execution
- technology-neutral diagnosis
- capability routing
- structured enterprise learning

That combination still needs market proof.
