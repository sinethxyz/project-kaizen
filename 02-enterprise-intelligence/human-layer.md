# Human Layer

Enterprises are human systems expressed partly through machines.

Model observable factors such as:

- trust
- morale
- communication patterns
- adoption behaviour
- risk perception
- identity
- loyalty
- fear of loss
- resistance to change
- informal norms

Avoid amateur psychological diagnosis. Prefer behavioural evidence.

Instead of `the founder is controlling`, record observations such as `material decisions are repeatedly escalated to the founder despite delegated authority`.
