# Founder Imprint

Founders often encode assumptions into the organisation long after those assumptions are explicitly discussed.

Model observable patterns:

- which decisions remain centralised
- which information the founder personally checks
- which risks are systematically avoided
- what behaviour receives attention or reward
- who is trusted with bad news
- which early processes survived scale
- which organisational structures reflect early-stage constraints

The purpose is not to psychoanalyse founders. It is to identify how founder-created path dependence affects the enterprise today.
