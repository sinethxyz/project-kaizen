# Historical Layer

Many enterprise problems are path-dependent.

Reconstruct how the company became what it is:

```text
FOUNDING
↓
EARLY PRODUCT / MARKET
↓
EARLY HIRING
↓
GROWTH
↓
SYSTEMS ADDED
↓
MANAGEMENT LAYERS
↓
ACQUISITIONS / CRISES / REGULATION
↓
TODAY
```

A process that looks irrational now may have been rational when created.

History helps distinguish deliberately designed complexity from organisational sediment.
