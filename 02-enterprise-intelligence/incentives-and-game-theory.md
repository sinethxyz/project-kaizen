# Incentives and Game Theory

An enterprise contains actors with overlapping but non-identical objectives.

Examples:

- CEO wants growth and strategic control
- CFO wants predictable economics
- CTO wants architectural integrity and delivery speed
- department leader wants resources and autonomy
- employee wants security, status, manageable workload, and meaning
- vendor wants contract expansion
- operator wants a successful transformation

A transformation changes payoffs.

Ask:

- who benefits?
- who loses control, status, budget, or optionality?
- who can block the change?
- what behaviour is rational under the current incentives even if it is bad for the enterprise?

The technically optimal intervention may not be organisationally executable without changing incentives.
