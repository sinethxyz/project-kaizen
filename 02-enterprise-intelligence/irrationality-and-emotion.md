# Irrationality and Emotion

Companies do not behave like perfectly rational optimisation functions.

Relevant forces include:

- fear
- ego
- identity
- status
- loyalty
- resentment
- sunk-cost attachment
- uncertainty avoidance
- social proof
- internal narratives
- loss aversion

These forces should be treated as possible explanatory variables, not excuses for speculation.

The model should prefer observable behaviour and multiple sources of evidence over confident claims about hidden motives.
