# Positioning

There are two different questions:

## What does the market understand today?

Likely: **AI modernisation / AI transformation**.

## What should define the company's internal intellectual boundary?

Potentially: **continuous enterprise adaptation**.

These do not need to be the same sentence.

A possible market-facing message is:

> We help companies modernise for the AI era. We do not begin with AI. We begin with the business, diagnose what actually needs to change, and deploy AI only where it creates measurable value.

This preserves a clear wedge without making AI the permanent worldview.
