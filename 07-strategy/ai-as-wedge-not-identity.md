# AI as Wedge, Not Identity

AI can be what Persist sells now without becoming what Persist permanently is.

```text
TODAY
AI modernisation
↓
UNDERLYING METHOD
enterprise understanding
→ diagnosis
→ routing
→ deployment
→ measurement
→ learning
↓
LONG-TERM MINDSET
continuous adaptation
```

This avoids a category trap: becoming so attached to the technology that originally created demand that the company resists the next shift.

AI expertise should still be deep. The distinction is that AI does not define the boundary of possible answers.
