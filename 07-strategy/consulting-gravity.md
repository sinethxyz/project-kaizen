# Consulting Gravity

Project Kaizen begins with people-heavy diagnosis and delivery. That creates a real risk of becoming a traditional labour-heavy consultancy with better language.

A possible escape path is:

```text
OPERATOR WORK
↓
REPETITION
↓
PATTERNS
↓
STANDARDISED METHOD
↓
INTERNAL TOOLS
↓
AUTOMATION
↓
SOFTWARE — WHERE JUSTIFIED
```

Do not force productisation before repetition exists.

The test is whether each new client merely consumes more labour or also improves reusable capability, speed, quality, and knowledge.
