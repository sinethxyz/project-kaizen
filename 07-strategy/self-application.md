# Apply Kaizen to Persist

Persist cannot preach adaptability while becoming emotionally attached to its own operating model.

Repeatedly ask:

- are customers changing?
- is diagnosis still valuable?
- is the routing network still efficient?
- can AI automate our own work?
- should an internal workflow become software?
- are we overdependent on a vendor?
- has the market moved?
- is today's revenue model becoming tomorrow's constraint?

If `diagnose → route → deploy` becomes inferior to another model, Persist should change it.

> **The company must never make its current method sacred.**
