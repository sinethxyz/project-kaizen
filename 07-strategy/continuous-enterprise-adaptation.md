# Continuous Enterprise Adaptation

The deeper strategic category is not necessarily `AI transformation`.

It is the capability to repeatedly understand what changed, what it means for the enterprise, what should change internally, and how to execute that change.

```text
ENVIRONMENT CHANGES
↓
ENTERPRISE BECOMES MISALIGNED
↓
DIAGNOSE
↓
ADAPT
↓
MEASURE
↓
LEARN
↺
```

This should begin as an internal doctrine. Whether it becomes an external market category should be decided by customer comprehension and evidence, not by elegance.
